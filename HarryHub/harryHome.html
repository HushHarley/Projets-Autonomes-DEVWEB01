Who is Harry Potter

Harry Potter is an orphan boy who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he's a wizard. Raised by his abusive aunt and uncle in the ordinary (Muggle) world, he has no idea about his magical heritage until he's invited to attend Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He's famous among those in the wizarding world for surviving an attack as a baby by the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, an event that left him with a lightning-bolt scar and a strange connection to his enemy.

Where the Story Takes Place

The series is set mostly in a hidden and magical version of Britain that exists alongside the everyday world. Most of the story unfolds at Hogwarts, a castle-school only accessed by the secret platform at King's Cross Station, platform 9 and three quarters, but it also begins to reach into wizarding neighborhoods, government offices, and eventually the whole magical world as Voldemort continues to seize power.

Full Summary

Across all the seven books, Harry goes from a clueless first-year into the main figure in the fight against Voldemort, who is trying to return to full power and take over the world. With his friends Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, Harry enjoys school life, uncovers secrets tied to his past, and gradually learns why Voldemort made him an enemy in the first place. The series builds toward an war between Voldemort's followers and everyone else, testing Harry's courage, loyalty, and identity along the way.
